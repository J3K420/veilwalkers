# Story 4.6: Apply encounter charges (Stability Boost / Nightveil Filter)

Status: done

<!-- Note: Validation is optional. Run validate-create-story for quality check before dev-story. -->

## Story

As a player,
I want to spend earned charges to shape an encounter,
So that I can improve tough odds without paying money.

## Acceptance Criteria

**AC-1 (Stability Boost — consumes ONE `StabilityBoost` charge, NEVER Credits, and raises Scan/Capture/Slay ease for the REMAINDER of the current encounter, in ONE atomic write).**
**Given** a live encounter with a settled Monster AND a balance of ≥ 1 `StabilityBoost` charge
**When** the player applies Stability Boost
**Then** EXACTLY ONE `StabilityBoost` charge is consumed (`model.StabilityBoostCharges -= 1`, via `ChargeInventory.SetCount`, the guarded `>= 0` rule), NO Credits are touched (the `model.Credits` field is byte-identical), AND the encounter records a "Stability Boost is active" modifier that raises the SUCCESS ease of subsequent Scan/Capture/Slay attempts for the remainder of THIS encounter — the charge decrement commits in ONE `SaveAsync` (AR-8), whole-mutation rollback on fault. The applied-modifier record is per-encounter (the `_scannedThisEncounter` precedent — in-memory, cleared on `EndEncounter`); the persisted-snapshot disk round-trip is Story 5.4.
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-4.6 (lines 709-711); docs/epics.md:33 (FR-10 — "Encounter consumables (XP charges) + Retry — Stability Boost and Nightveil Filter each consume one XP-earned charge (never Credits, blocked at zero, never negative)"); docs/architecture.md:75-76 (leveling up grants Strong Capture / Stability Boost / Nightveil Filter charges); docs/architecture.md:492-493 ("Charge consumption is atomic: mutate-in-memory → persist → rollback on failure"); docs/architecture.md:505 ("charges for extras via Progression"); CLAUDE.md Economy canon ("Extras are XP-earned charges, never purchasable: Strong Capture, Stability Boost, Nightveil Filter … consumed one charge per use"). `ChargeType.StabilityBoost` (ChargeType.cs:21), `ChargeInventory.GetCount/SetCount` (the ONE mapping to `SaveModel.StabilityBoostCharges`), and the consume-one-charge atomic-write pattern (the 4.4 `CommitCaptureAsync` StrongCapture consume) ALREADY EXIST. **Decision A (below): the "ease raised for the remainder of the encounter" is recorded as an in-encounter modifier flag (like scan progress); the magnitude of the ease bonus is provisional OQ-9 balancing, NOT an AC — the AC is "a charge is consumed (never Credits) AND ease is raised for the rest of the encounter."**]

**AC-2 (Nightveil Filter — consumes ONE `NightveilFilter` charge, NEVER Credits, applies an atmospheric visual filter AND grants a small rarity boost, in ONE atomic write).**
**Given** a live encounter AND a balance of ≥ 1 `NightveilFilter` charge
**When** the player applies Nightveil Filter
**Then** EXACTLY ONE `NightveilFilter` charge is consumed (never Credits), the encounter records a "Nightveil Filter is active" modifier that (a) flags the atmospheric visual filter for the render layer (the actual filter VFX is Epic 6 — 4.6 records the logical state) AND (b) raises the rarity of subsequent Lure rolls for the remainder of THIS encounter (the "small rarity boost") — the charge decrement commits in ONE `SaveAsync` (AR-8), whole-mutation rollback on fault. The applied-modifier record is per-encounter (in-memory, cleared on `EndEncounter`; the disk snapshot is Story 5.4).
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-4.6 (lines 713-715); docs/epics.md:33 (FR-10); docs/architecture.md:75-76; CLAUDE.md ("Nightveil Filter … granted by leveling up … consumed one charge per use"). `ChargeType.NightveilFilter` (ChargeType.cs:24) + `ChargeInventory` mapping to `SaveModel.NightveilFilterCharges` ALREADY EXIST. **Decision A: the rarity-boost magnitude + the visual-filter VFX are provisional/Epic-6; the AC is "a charge is consumed (never Credits) AND a visual filter is flagged AND a small rarity boost is granted for the rest of the encounter."**]

**AC-3 (zero charges → blocked with an "earn via XP" hint; the count NEVER goes negative; NO Credits touched).**
**Given** ZERO charges of an extra (Stability Boost or Nightveil Filter)
**When** the player attempts to apply it
**Then** the attempt is BLOCKED — NO charge is decremented (the count stays at 0, NEVER negative — the `ChargeInventory.SetCount` guarded `>= 0` rule + the gated decrement), NO Credits are touched, NO persist occurs (save-count 0), NO in-encounter modifier is recorded, and a typed `InsufficientCharges` failure is returned carrying the "earn via XP" signal (the extras are XP-earned, never purchasable — the result distinguishes "blocked: no charge" from "blocked: not settled"). The encounter stays live.
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-4.6 (lines 717-719 — "blocked with an 'earn via XP' hint and the count never goes negative"); docs/epics.md:33 (FR-10 — "blocked at zero, never negative"); docs/architecture.md:75 ("granted by leveling up … never purchasable"). The 4.4 `CommitCaptureAsync` zero-charge block (`InsufficientCharges`, no mutation/persist/event, the count can never go negative because the only decrement is guarded by `priorChargeCount == 0`) is the EXACT precedent — mirror it. `SpendFailureReason.InsufficientCharges` (Core) ALREADY EXISTS.]

**AC-4 (a level-up that grants a charge MID-ENCOUNTER makes the new charge usable IMMEDIATELY in that same encounter).**
**Given** a live encounter AND a level-up (e.g. from a Capture/Slay XP grant) that grants a `StabilityBoost`/`NightveilFilter` charge
**When** the level-up happens during the encounter
**Then** the newly granted charge is usable IMMEDIATELY in that same encounter — applying the extra right after reads the LIVE charge count from the persisted `SaveModel` (no stale snapshot of charges is cached in the encounter), so the just-granted charge is spendable without ending/re-entering the encounter.
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-4.6 (lines 721-723 — "the new charge is usable immediately in that same encounter"); docs/architecture.md:75-76 (level-up grants charges). This is naturally satisfied by reading the charge count from `RequireModel()`/`ChargeInventory.GetCount` AT APPLY TIME (the `CommitCaptureAsync` precedent reads `ChargeInventory.GetCount(model, ...)` live inside the locked write — it does NOT snapshot charges at encounter start). **Decision B (below): the apply path MUST NOT cache the charge count at encounter start — it reads live from the model, so a mid-encounter grant is immediately visible. Pin a test: a Capture that crosses a level threshold granting a `StabilityBoost` charge, then an immediate apply succeeds (consuming the just-granted charge) within the SAME encounter.**]

## Dev Notes

### The six atomic-write siblings (4.6 adds the sixth)
EncounterService hosts a family of composed writers, all sharing the AR-8 pipeline (acquire `SaveMutationLock` once → capture `_saveService.Current` once → mutate in memory → ONE `SaveAsync` → recovery-swap guard → whole-mutation rollback on fault → events after release):
1. `CommitActionAsync` (4.1) — charge consume + codex discovery (the generic primitive; `RunActionAsync` drives it). **NOT used by the extras** (it ALWAYS stages a `StageDiscovery`; an extra consumes a charge but discovers NOTHING — there is no "no-discovery" `DiscoverySource`, and an extra must not discover a Monster as a side effect).
2. `CommitLureSpendAsync` (4.2) — credit spend, no codex, no XP.
3. `CommitScanAsync` (4.3) — FREE, scan-flag/encounter-state record, no economy. **Its in-encounter-record + rollback pattern (`_scannedThisEncounter`) is the model for the extras' `_activeExtras` modifier record.**
4. `CommitCaptureAsync` (4.4) — a ROLL-aware write: charge (Strong) + codex + XP, roll-gated. **Its StrongCapture charge-consume slice (consume-on-attempt, the zero-charge block, the flag-before-mutation revert) is the model for the extras' charge-consume slice.**
5. `CommitSlayAsync` (4.5) — a ROLL-gated write: credits + codex (`Slain`) + XP, committed only on a winning roll.
6. **`CommitApplyExtraAsync` (4.6, NEW)** — a charge-only write: ONE `StabilityBoost`/`NightveilFilter` charge consumed (no Credits, no codex, no XP) + an in-encounter modifier recorded. Its delta — a charge consume WITHOUT a codex discovery, plus an in-memory per-encounter modifier — matches no existing sibling, so it gets its own write.

**Why the extras are a NEW sibling, not a reuse of `CommitActionAsync` (settles the 4.1/4.4 "extras may compose CommitActionAsync" deferral):** the 4.1/4.4 deferrals left extras to decide whether to compose `RunActionAsync`/`CommitActionAsync`. The answer is NO for Stability Boost / Nightveil Filter: `CommitActionAsync` ALWAYS stages a Codex discovery (`StageDiscovery`), but an extra discovers nothing — it consumes a charge and records an in-encounter modifier. So contorting an extra through `CommitActionAsync` would require a bogus `DiscoverySource` and would discover a Monster as a side effect of applying a Boost (a correctness bug). The extras get their own composed write, reusing the charge-consume SLICE shape from `CommitCaptureAsync` and the in-encounter-record SLICE shape from `CommitScanAsync`. (`RunActionAsync`/`CommitActionAsync` remain the generic charge+discovery primitive — Strong Capture already flows through the 4.4 Capture path; no remaining caller of `RunActionAsync` lands in 4.6, so update its "extras 4.6 compose this" section comment to reflect that the two non-capture extras do NOT compose it.)

**The charge-only-no-discovery wrinkle (the AC-1/AC-2 heart):** unlike every prior sibling, the extras commit a charge decrement with NO codex and NO XP. The in-encounter modifier (`_activeExtras`) is the SECOND slice — an in-memory record (the `_scannedThisEncounter` precedent), reverted on a fault, cleared on `EndEncounter`. The disk snapshot of the active modifiers (across a Shop round-trip) is Story 5.4 (the same deferral as the scan-progress snapshot).

**AC-4 (mid-encounter grant) is free if Decision B holds:** the apply path reads the charge count LIVE from `RequireModel()` inside the locked write (the `CommitCaptureAsync` `ChargeInventory.GetCount(model, ...)` precedent) — it does NOT snapshot charges at encounter start. So a charge granted by a mid-encounter level-up is immediately visible to the next apply. The ONLY way to break AC-4 is to cache the count at encounter start — don't.
