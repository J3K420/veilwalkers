# Story 4.4: Capture a Monster (free base / Strong Capture)

Status: done

<!-- Note: Validation is optional. Run validate-create-story for quality check before dev-story. -->

## Story

As a player,
I want to capture Monsters gently, optionally boosting my odds with an earned charge,
So that I can fill the Codex.

## Acceptance Criteria

**AC-1 (base Capture is FREE; a SUCCESS adds/updates the Codex entry and increments X/67 if newly discovered; Credits never change).**
**Given** a settled Monster in a live encounter
**When** the player taps Capture (base, free) and the attempt SUCCEEDS
**Then** the Monster's Codex entry is added/updated via Epic 2's discovery (the `Captured` flag set, `OnMonsterDiscovered` raised + `DiscoveredCount` incremented IFF it is the first discovery), AND the Credit balance never changes (base Capture costs no Credits and no charges).
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-4.4 (lines 671-673); docs/epics.md:31 (FR-8 — "base attempt free; … success creates/updates Codex entry"); docs/architecture.md:68 ("Scan and base Capture remain free"); docs/architecture.md:74-77 (Capture grants XP; charges are the earned extras); CLAUDE.md Economy canon ("Scan and base Capture are free" — Credits buy ONLY Basic/Premium/Multi Lure + Slay). The discovery primitive `CodexService.StageDiscovery(model, id, DiscoverySource.Capture)` ALREADY EXISTS (CodexService.cs:358) and creates the key + sets `Captured` + raises `OnMonsterDiscovered`/`OnCodexCompleted` on first discovery; `DiscoverySource.Capture` ALREADY EXISTS (DiscoverySource.cs).]

**AC-2 (Strong Capture consumes EXACTLY ONE charge — never Credits — applies a HIGHER success probability; blocked at zero charges with an "earn via XP" hint; never goes negative).**
**Given** the player chooses Strong Capture
**When** they have ≥1 Strong-Capture charge
**Then** exactly one `ChargeType.StrongCapture` charge is consumed (never Credits), and a success probability STRICTLY HIGHER than the free base attempt is applied
**And** with zero charges the Strong Capture is BLOCKED before any spend (a typed `InsufficientCharges` failure carrying an "earn via XP" affordance), the charge count never goes negative, and the encounter stays active.
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-4.4 (lines 675-678); docs/epics.md:31 (FR-8 — "Strong Capture consumes one XP-earned charge to raise success (never Credits, blocked at zero charges, never negative)"); docs/architecture.md:75-77 (leveling grants charges; "using an extra consumes one charge (no money cost ever)"); docs/architecture.md:548 (a Strong Capture charge is earned, never on-demand — the earned-progression curve). The charge store + the never-negative guard ALREADY EXIST: `SaveModel.StrongCaptureCharges` (SaveModel.cs:62) + `ChargeInventory.GetCount`/`SetCount` (ChargeInventory.cs:29-85, `SetCount` THROWS on a negative count — line 64). The zero-charge block precedent is `CommitActionAsync` (EncounterService.cs:727-732, returns `InsufficientCharges` with no mutation). **DESIGN DECISION (Decision A below): is the charge consumed on a FAILED Strong Capture roll? Resolution: YES — the charge buys the improved ODDS of the attempt, so it is spent on the attempt regardless of the roll outcome (epics.md:31 "consumes one charge to raise success"; architecture.md:77 "using an extra consumes one charge"). See Decision A.**]

**AC-3 (a FAILED Capture leaves the encounter active and offers a FREE Retry; no Codex discovery is recorded on failure).**
**Given** a settled Monster
**When** a Capture attempt (base OR Strong) FAILS the success roll
**Then** NO Codex discovery is recorded (no key created, no `Captured` flag set, `OnMonsterDiscovered` NOT raised, `DiscoveredCount` unchanged), the encounter stays active (returns to `Lured`, NOT ended), and a free Retry is offered (re-attempt at no Credit cost — Retry is just calling Capture again).
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-4.4 (lines 680-682 — "a failed Capture … the encounter stays active and a free Retry is offered (re-attempt, no Credit cost)"); docs/epics.md:31 (FR-8 — "failed Capture leaves Encounter active and offers free Retry"); docs/architecture.md:66-67 ("Retry is free — re-attempting just means luring again … no separate Retry credit cost"); docs/architecture.md:326-327 ("a failed Capture/Slay leaves the Encounter active and re-enables the action; Retry is free"). The state-machine "failed action returns to Lured for retry" is ALREADY PROVEN by 4.1's representative (EncounterServiceTests `In_encounter_capture_with_no_charge_fails_and_returns_to_Lured_for_retry`); 4.4 extends it to a FAILED-ROLL (not just a no-charge) failure. **CRITICAL: a failed roll must NOT stage a discovery — the inherited `CommitActionAsync` ALWAYS stages a discovery, so 4.4 introduces a path that consumes the charge (for Strong, Decision A) WITHOUT staging a discovery on a roll failure. See Decision C. NOTE (verified): `EncounterStateMachine.CompleteResolve` AND `FailResolve` BOTH transition to `Lured` (EncounterStateMachine.cs:77, :85) — the encounter stays active either way. A failed ROLL is a SUCCESSFULLY-RESOLVED "miss", so it uses `CompleteResolve` (a clean completion for `OnStateChanged`/deferred-suspend H2 consumers), NOT because `FailResolve` would strand/end the encounter (it would not). See Decision C/E.**]

**AC-4 (a SUCCESSFUL Capture grants XP — atomically with the charge/discovery, in ONE save).**
**Given** a Capture attempt that SUCCEEDS (base or Strong)
**When** the success commits
**Then** the player-wide XP pool is increased by the per-Capture XP amount (`EconomyConfig.xpPerCapture`) AS PART OF the SAME single atomic write that records the discovery (and, for Strong, consumes the charge) — one `SaveAsync`, whole-mutation rollback on fault — and crossing a level threshold grants charges via the inherited progression rules. A FAILED Capture grants NO XP. Credits never change.
[Source: docs/architecture.md:74 ("Single player-wide XP/level pool. Capture and Slay both grant XP; Slay grants more"); docs/architecture.md:187-188 (the SaveModel stores player XP + level + per-extra charge counts); CLAUDE.md Economy canon ("Capture/Slay earn XP; Slay earns more"). `EconomyConfig.xpPerCapture` ALREADY EXISTS (EconomyConfig.cs, the `SetForTests` arg `xpPerCapture`). **CRITICAL ATOMICITY CONFLICT (verified): `ProgressionService.AddXpAsync` (ProgressionService.cs:76) takes the SAME `_mutationLock` AND does its OWN `SaveAsync` (line 156). So a Capture that calls `AddXpAsync` AFTER its own composed write = TWO saves (violates AR-8 "one persist per action") and cannot re-enter the lock. 4.4 MUST fold the XP grant INTO the composed capture write (one locked section, one `SaveAsync`). See Decision F — this is the meatiest part of 4.4 and the spec OWNS the resolution, not the dev.**]

## Dev Notes

### The four atomic-write siblings (4.4 adds the fourth)
EncounterService now hosts a family of composed writers, all sharing the AR-8 pipeline (acquire `SaveMutationLock` once → capture `_saveService.Current` once → mutate in memory → ONE `SaveAsync` → recovery-swap guard → whole-mutation rollback on fault → events after release):
1. `CommitActionAsync` (4.1) — charge consume + codex discovery (the representative; 4.4 may repurpose it).
2. `CommitLureSpendAsync` (4.2) — credit spend, no codex.
3. `CommitScanAsync` (4.3) — FREE, scan-flag/encounter-state, no economy.
4. **`CommitCaptureAsync` (4.4, NEW)** — a ROLL-aware write: optionally consume a Strong charge (Decision A: on the attempt, win or lose), optionally stage a discovery (ONLY on a winning roll), with a no-op short-circuit for a base-fail (nothing changed → no persist).

**The new wrinkle vs 4.1-4.3:** the prior siblings each had a DETERMINISTIC delta. Capture's delta depends on a probabilistic roll computed BEFORE the write (in `CaptureSystem`, the `LureSystem` rarity-roll precedent). Compute the roll, THEN compose the write with the known outcome — do not roll inside the locked section (mirror 4.2: `LureSystem` rolls, `EncounterService` composes the spend with the result).
