# Story 5.1: Browse and purchase Credit Packs via Google Play Billing

Status: done

<!-- Note: Validation is optional. Run validate-create-story for quality check before dev-story. -->

## Story

As a player,
I want to buy Credit Packs through the official store,
So that I can top up cleanly when I want more Credits.

## Acceptance Criteria

**AC-1 (the catalog lists at least the three launch packs with localized prices + transparent contents — no gacha/mystery-box).**
**Given** the Shop (`CreditPackCatalog` + UI)
**When** it renders
**Then** it lists at least the three launch packs — Starter (50 / $1.99), Hunter (150 + 20 bonus / $4.99, "POPULAR"), Veil (400 + 100 bonus + Guaranteed-Rare Lure / $9.99, "BEST VALUE") — with prices localized by Play and transparent contents (no gacha/mystery-box).
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-5.1 (AC-1, lines 762-764); CLAUDE.md Credit-packs canon: 50/$1.99 · 150+20/$4.99 · 400+100+Guaranteed-Rare Lure/$9.99; docs/architecture.md:507 (FR-13 → Billing/* + UI/Shop). **Localized prices come from the store adapter (Play returns the formatted localized price string); the $-amounts above are the fallback/reference USD, NOT hard-coded display values.** **Transparent contents = the pack's base + bonus + any one-shot item are declared in `CreditPackCatalog`; no randomized/hidden reward.**]

**AC-2 (purchases route EXCLUSIVELY through Google Play Billing — Unity IAP 5 / Play Billing 8; no alternative payment UI anywhere).**
**Given** a purchase
**When** it routes
**Then** it goes exclusively through Google Play Billing (Unity IAP 5 / Play Billing 8); no alternative payment UI exists anywhere in the app.
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-5.1 (AC-2, lines 766-768); docs/architecture.md:86 (Billing: Google Play Billing Library, no third-party payment anywhere), :121-130 (legacy plugin REJECTED; Unity IAP 5 the only compliant path; bundles Play Billing 8.0.0), :146 (Unity IAP 5 = Play Billing 8.0.0); CLAUDE.md Hard requirements: "All purchases via official Google Play Billing — no third-party payment." **STRUCTURAL AC:** the real-money call lives ONLY behind the Billing module's store-adapter seam; no other module references a payment SDK or presents a pay UI.]

**AC-3 (a successful purchase increments the balance by base+bonus and raises `OnPurchaseCompleted`; the rest of the app calls only `BillingService.PurchaseAsync(packId)` + listens for the event — Billing → Economy stays one-way).**
**Given** a successful purchase
**When** it completes
**Then** the balance increments by the pack total (base + bonus) and `OnPurchaseCompleted` is raised; the rest of the app calls only `BillingService.PurchaseAsync(packId)` and listens for the event (Billing → Economy stays one-way).
[Source: docs/epics.md#Story-5.1 (AC-3, lines 770-772); docs/architecture.md:495-496 (Billing boundary — all real-money flow stays in Billing; the rest of the app calls `BillingService.PurchaseAsync(packId)` + listens for `OnPurchaseCompleted`), :478-480 (Billing → Economy strictly one-way — `CreditService.GrantCreditsAsync` is the seam; services never call back into Billing), :522 (Purchase data flow: UI → BillingService → Unity IAP → PurchaseReconciler → Economy). **The grant goes through `ICreditService.GrantCreditsAsync(int)` (the existing one-way seam, CreditService.cs:149 — already anticipates "a 5.2 reconciliation replay").**]

## Dev Notes

### SCOPE — read this first
This is the **first Billing/Monetization story** — it establishes the `Veilwalkers.Billing` module (currently just an empty asmdef) by mirroring the AR module's adapter pattern. It builds: the transparent data-driven `CreditPackCatalog`, the `IStoreAdapter` seam (the untestable Unity-IAP edge) + its stub-safe editor adapter + a test fake, and `BillingService.PurchaseAsync(packId)` + `OnPurchaseCompleted` that grants base+bonus ONE-WAY into Economy via `GrantCreditsAsync`. It DEFERS exactly-once reconciliation to 5.2, the Guaranteed-Rare grant to 5.3, the Shop UI to Epic 6, and the device IAP SDK glue to the device build.

1. **`CreditPackCatalog`** (architecture-named) — the three launch packs as transparent, declared data (Starter / Hunter POPULAR / Veil BEST VALUE), every grant a field, no gacha. Localized prices come from Play at runtime, never in-code.
2. **`IStoreAdapter` seam** — the Unity-IAP edge behind a thin adapter (the `IArAnchorProvider` precedent): all DECISIONS in `BillingService`, the untestable SDK call in `UnityIapStoreAdapter` (`#if UNITY_ANDROID && !UNITY_EDITOR`, stub-safe editor path), a `FakeStoreAdapter` for headless tests.

### The design decisions this story locks (call them out for the CR reviewer)
1. **The Billing module mirrors the AR module's adapter split (Decision A/B).** Untestable SDK edge (`UnityIapStoreAdapter`, `#if UNITY_ANDROID && !UNITY_EDITOR`, stub-safe `#else`) behind a thin seam (`IStoreAdapter`); all DECISIONS (catalog lookup, grant-on-success, one-way-to-Economy, the failure taxonomy) live in the pure-logic `BillingService`, headless-tested against `FakeStoreAdapter`. This is the literal answer to the Epic-4-retro "Billing seam/adapter split" scope item.
2. **Billing → Economy is STRICTLY one-way via `ICreditService.GrantCreditsAsync` (AC-3, architecture.md:478-480).** `BillingService` calls Economy; Economy never references Billing (the asmdef edge is Billing→Economy, never the reverse — structurally enforced by the acyclicity test). The grant uses the existing `GrantCreditsAsync(int)` (CreditService.cs:149) — which ALREADY anticipates a "5.2 reconciliation replay" in its `checked` comment, confirming this is the intended seam.
3. **5.1 is the SIMPLE grant-once-on-success path; 5.2 layers exactly-once (NFR-4) on top (Decision B step-6 / Decision C).** 5.1 grants `base+bonus` once per successful `PurchaseAsync`, surfaces `GrantFailed` (paid-but-not-durably-saved) + `AlreadyOwned` as distinct typed reasons (the 5.2 seams), and pins "no double-grant in the happy path" — but does NOT survive interruption or dedup by order id. The `playOrderId` is carried on the store result NOW because 5.2 keys dedup on it.
4. **The catalog is transparent + data-driven (AC-1).** Every grantable thing (base, bonus, the Guaranteed-Rare flag) is a declared field on `CreditPack` — no randomized/hidden reward (Teen-rating + no-gacha canon). Pack definitions are in-code canon (not balancing tunables, unlike `EconomyConfig` costs); localized PRICES are NEVER in-code (Play returns them at runtime).
5. **`BillingService` registers LIVE at boot (Decision D), unlike the `.asset`-blocked services.** No unauthored-asset blocker (catalog in-code, adapter editor path a no-op). The Shop UI consumer is Epic 6 (the UI-presenter altitude split, [[ui-presenter-pattern]]) — it resolves `IBillingService` via `GameServices.Get` + degrades while unplaced.
6. **The Guaranteed-Rare Lure is DECLARED, not granted, in 5.1.** The Veil pack carries `IncludesGuaranteedRareLure = true` for catalog transparency; the grant/consume is Story 5.3 (the reserved `LureKind.GuaranteedRare` + `RarityThresholds.GuaranteedRareFloor`).

### Edge cases & boundary rules (must-handle — these become test cases)
- **Successful purchase (AC-3):** balance += base+bonus in ONE `GrantCreditsAsync` (save-count 1), `OnPurchaseCompleted` once, `PurchaseResult.Success(newBalance)`.
- **Cancelled / Failed store result (AC-3):** balance unchanged (save-count 0), no event, typed `Cancelled`/`StoreUnavailable`.
- **Unknown pack id (AC-2):** typed `UnknownPack`, NO store-adapter call (counter 0), no grant, no event.
- **Grant-persist fault (Decision B step-6):** store `Purchased` but `FailNextSave` → `GrantCreditsAsync` rolls back → `GrantFailed`, no event, balance net-unchanged (the 5.2 reconciliation seam).
- **AlreadyOwned (Decision C):** typed `AlreadyOwned`, no grant, no event, logged (5.2 handles the consume/grant).
- **No double-grant (NFR-4 happy path):** one completed purchase → exactly one grant (save-count 1, event once). Mutation: a second grant → red.
- **Ctor null args / no locator:** every ctor arg null → `ArgumentNullException`; no `GameServices.Get` in Billing.
- **Catalog transparency (AC-1):** every pack's grants are declared fields; Veil's Guaranteed-Rare flag true, others false; badges correct; no randomized reward.
- **Editor-no-store (NFR-3):** the `#else` `UnityIapStoreAdapter` editor path returns a deterministic non-crashing result + logs via `GameLog` (no throw at boot or on a purchase attempt in-editor).
